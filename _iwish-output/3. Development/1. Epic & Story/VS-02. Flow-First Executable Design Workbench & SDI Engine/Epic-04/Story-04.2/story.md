## 1. Story Description & Context
Trong đợt tái cấu trúc & đối chiếu `master` của Epic 04, Story 04.2 đóng vai trò lát cắt **Persistence V7, Dual-Ingress Kernel & Managed FS Boundary (`CS = 5.5`)**, cập nhật thẳng theo thực tế `V5/V6` đã checksum-locked trên `master`:
1. **Nâng cấp cộng dồn V7 (`V7__epic04_cas_seq_and_vault_convergence.sql`, `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION = 7`):** Trên `master`, `V5` và `V6__epic04_complete_contracts.sql` đã triển khai và khóa checksum (`pool.rs:569`, `lockfile.rs:14`), nên tuyệt đối **không** sửa lại file V5/V6 cũ. Thay vào đó V7 thực thi nâng cấp cộng dồn idempotent với `PRAGMA table_info` guards cho `chat_sessions.archived_at` & `chat_message_node_scopes.tenant_id`, thêm `execution_events.project_seq` (`UNIQUE(project_id, project_seq)`) + `composite_scope_key`, rebuild 12-bước bảng `node_execution_jobs` (bổ sung `'PAUSED' | 'INTERRUPTED'`, `composite_scope_key`, `daemon_nonce`, `updated_at`), thêm `element_comments.artifact_sha256` + `anchor_kind`, `agent_settings` (`permission_mode`, `vault_mode`, `onboarding_done`), và `node_chat_sessions` (`composite_scope_key`, `unread_count`, `last_message_id`), đồng thời nâng `lockfile.rs` `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION = 7`.
2. **Bảo toàn SSE Streaming & dọn nốt RAM `new Map()` trong Vite BFF:** Commit `381dd87` trên `master` đã nâng `vite-bff-plugin.ts#sendWebResponse` dùng `Readable.fromWeb` và chuyển route comments/fixtures khỏi RAM `Map`. Story 04.2 giữ kiểm thử hồi quy cho phần này, đồng thời xóa sạch `new Map()` còn sót ở `sdi/intent` & `proposals`, nối đủ BFF `DELETE /api/projects/:id` & `POST /api/projects/:id/restore`, và gắn `X-Cowok-Session-Token` qua `strict-loopback`.
3. **Thay `showDirectoryPicker` bằng Managed Root 1-Click + Hardened Promote Jail:** Bỏ hoàn toàn `window.showDirectoryPicker()`, mặc định 1-click tạo workspace an toàn tại `~/CowokProjects/<slug>` (`^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9_-]{0,62}$`, override qua `COWOK_PROJECTS_ROOT` cho test) và dùng `GET /api/v1/fs/browse` khi trỏ codebase sẵn có; đồng thời gia cố `handle_promote_scratch` (`legacy_sessions.rs:487-532`) với **`O_NOFOLLOW` + deepest-ancestor `(ancestor_dev, ancestor_ino)` anchoring (`mkdirat`/`openat`) + danh sách chặn sau `canonicalize()`** (`~/.cowok-design`, `~/.ssh`, `~/.gnupg`, `~/.aws`, `~/.kube`, `/etc`, exact `/private`, `/private/etc`, `/private/var/db`, `/private/var/root` — cho phép `/private/tmp` và `/private/var/folders` của macOS) và **HMAC `PathToken` đã xác thực `(daemon_nonce, canonical_path, ancestor_dev_ino)`** — **fail closed** (tuyệt đối không fallback `./projects/*`). Mọi lời gọi client đi qua `src/api/harness-daemon.ts` (`apiHarnessDaemon`).

*(Lưu ý: Day-0 Onboarding CLI Doctor & BYOK Vault thuộc `Story 04.3`, Keyed Parallel Coordinator thuộc `Story 04.4`, và State-Hydrated Live Preview UI thuộc `Story 04.5`.)*

## 3. Acceptance Criteria (AC)
- **AC-01 (SQLite `V7__epic04_cas_seq_and_vault_convergence.sql` Additive Migration & `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION = 7`):** When upgrading any database (V0..V6), the daemon must preserve deployed V5/V6 checksums byte-for-byte, apply V7 idempotently with `PRAGMA table_info` guards (`chat_sessions.archived_at`, `chat_message_node_scopes.tenant_id` backfilled from `projects.tenant_id`), offset-safe `execution_events.project_seq` (`UNIQUE(project_id, project_seq)` + `composite_scope_key` used by `Last-Event-ID` SSE cursor), `PRAGMA foreign_keys = OFF` (`== 0`) + `TransactionBehavior::Exclusive` 12-step rebuild of `node_execution_jobs` (`'PAUSED' | 'INTERRUPTED'`, `composite_scope_key`, `daemon_nonce`, `updated_at`, preserving FKs to `projects`/`chat_sessions`), partial unique Trash-aware indexes on active `projects(slug)`/`projects(workspace_root)` AND global NULL-scoped unique indexes on `agent_settings` & `mcp_servers` (`WHERE project_id IS NULL`), `element_comments.artifact_sha256` + `anchor_kind`, `agent_settings` (`permission_mode`, `vault_mode`, `onboarding_done`), and `node_chat_sessions` (`composite_scope_key`, `unread_count`, `last_message_id`); and enforce `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION = 7` in `lockfile.rs` and `daemon-client.ts` (`schema_version < 7`).
- **AC-02 (Zero `new Map()` in `src/app/api/**`, BFF Regression & Project Delete/Restore/FS Routes):** When handling BFF requests across Vite BFF and Next App API, `vite-bff-plugin.ts#sendWebResponse` must stream `text/event-stream` via `Readable.fromWeb` without buffering, `src/app/api/**` (`sdi/intent`, `proposals`, `comments`, `fixtures`) must maintain 0 `new Map()` RAM stores, `vite-bff-routes.ts` must expose guarded forwarders for `DELETE /api/projects/:id`, `POST /api/projects/:id/restore`, `GET /api/fs/browse`, `POST /api/projects/:id/verify-path-token`, and `POST /api/projects/:id/promote`, `vite-bff-bootstrap.ts` must deny same-origin port fallback whenever active `bootCapTokens` exist and reject empty `Bearer` headers (`401`), and every `Dual-Ingress` call must enforce `strict-loopback` + `X-Cowok-Session-Token` while preserving JSON payloads.
- **AC-03 (Multi-Project CRUD, Trash Journal, F5 Hydration & Honest Workbench Prefs via `src/api/`):** When a user creates, switches, renames, soft-deletes (`DELETE /api/v1/projects/:id` setting `trash_deleted_at`), or restores (`POST /api/v1/projects/:id/restore`) a project, or reloads (`F5`) the Workbench, all state must persist in and rehydrate from SQLite V7 (`GET /api/v1/projects?include_trashed=true`) via `src/api/harness-daemon.ts` (`apiHarnessDaemon`) with collision-safe Trash slug reuse and restore handling (`zero` silent `./projects/*` demo fallbacks; zero sensitive `cowok_storage_mode`/`cowok_permission_level` flags in `localStorage`; zero fake `"ONLINE"`/`"CONNECTED"` MCP cards; and honest syntax/config verification on `/verify-key` & `/api/doctor` reserving live upstream HTTP/Keychain/Argon2id/16-CLI execution for `Story 04.3`).
- **AC-04 (`[EDGE-CASE]` `EC-PATH-HMAC-002`: 1-Click `~/CowokProjects/<slug>`, Daemon FS Browser & `O_NOFOLLOW` / Hardlink-Safe Promote Jail):** When choosing or promoting a workspace folder, the UI must eliminate `window.showDirectoryPicker()` completely, default 1-click to `~/CowokProjects/<slug>` (`^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9_-]{0,62}$`) + `GET /api/v1/fs/browse` (`O_NOFOLLOW`), and `POST /api/v1/projects/:id/path-token`, `verify-path-token`, `promote`, and `POST /api/v1/projects` must enforce `O_NOFOLLOW` + deepest-existing-ancestor `(ancestor_dev, ancestor_ino)` anchoring (`mkdirat`/`openat`/`fdopendir`), atomic `openat(O_CREAT|O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW)` + `renameat` rejecting destination hardlinks (`st_nlink > 1`) while tolerating legitimate source workspace hardlinks (`git objects`, `pnpm store`), and post-`canonicalize()` blocklist (`~/.cowok-design`, `~/.ssh`, `~/.gnupg`, `~/.aws`, `~/.kube`, `/etc`, exact `/private`, `/private/etc`, `/private/var/db`, `/private/var/root`, permitting `/private/tmp` and `/private/var/folders`), failing closed (`400/403/404`) with rollback cleanup on copy failures.
