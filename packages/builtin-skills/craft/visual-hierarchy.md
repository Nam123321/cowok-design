# Craft Rule: Visual Hierarchy & Focal Distribution

## Principles
1. **Single Primary Focal Point**: Every view or card must have exactly one primary call-to-action or focal anchor.
2. **Three Contrast Levels**: Establish clear High (Primary title & action), Medium (Body and secondary labels), and Low (Dividers, borders, subtle metadata) contrast tiers.
3. **Card Depth Limit**: Maximum card nesting depth is 2 levels. Avoid card-in-card-in-card nesting slop.
4. **Scannability**: Readers scan in F-patterns or Z-patterns. Key decisions must live in the top-left or prominent header.
