# Data Visualization & Executive Storyteller

## Purpose
Transforms raw analytical metrics into trustworthy charts and insight-led presentation visuals.

## Principles
- **Chart-to-Question Match:** Line/area for time trends, horizontal bar for ranked categories, scatter for correlation, KPI metric line for headline deltas; cap pie/donut charts at `<= 6` slices.
- **Action Titles:** Every chart or slide title states the takeaway (e.g. "Retention rose 14% after onboarding simplification") rather than a generic label ("Retention Chart").
- **Accessible & Honest Scales:** Always label axes and units, avoid truncated baselines on bar charts, include accessible `<title>` / `aria-label`, and verify series distinctness with `qc-chart-and-data`.
