# cowok.ai Logo Guideline v4.3

Status: **APPROVED HIGH-RES RASTER MASTER**

## Canonical source
All v4.3 logo variants derive from the single user-approved horizontal master and approved symbol source in `/logo/reference/`.

## Production family
- Horizontal Light / Dark — with and without tagline — 6000×2000
- Stacked Light / Dark — with and without tagline — 5000×5000
- Symbol-only transparent — 4096px long edge
- Wordmark Navy / White transparent — 4096px long edge
- App icons Light / Dark — 2048×2048
- Favicons — 16 / 32 / 64 / 128 / 256 / 512
- Mono symbol — Black / White
- Mono horizontal — Black / Reverse White
- Dedicated print-raster masters

## Usage
- Use horizontal lockup for web, documents, proposals and signage.
- Use stacked lockup for portrait/square layouts and merchandise.
- Use symbol-only for app icon, favicon, compact nav and social avatar.
- Use mono for engraving, stamps, thermal, embroidery references and one-color print.

## Minimum size
- Symbol: 24px absolute minimum; 32px preferred.
- Horizontal lockup: 120px wide minimum digital.
- Print horizontal: 30mm minimum.

## Clear space
Maintain at least ~0.5× symbol height around the logo; use 0.75–1× for premium/signage use.

## Color
- Blue: `#0057FF`
- Cyan: `#00D4FF`
- Orange: `#FF8A00`
- Navy: `#081E3A`

Do not recolor the official artwork locally.

## Wordmark
The `cowok.ai` wordmark is protected artwork. Do not re-typeset it with generic Geist or another font.

## Tagline
`Human + AI. One Workflow.`

Tagline in v4.3 production rasters is rendered cleanly from Inter Medium as the closest runtime implementation font available during export. Product typography remains Geist per Visual Foundations.

## Don't
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate or compress.
- Do not add glow, shadow, bevel, outline or 3D effects.
- Do not crop the brand board to use as production artwork.
- Do not auto-trace the raster and call it the final vector master.

## Vector status
No contour-traced or fake vector master is included in v4.3. The high-resolution PNG family is the canonical production artwork for this release.
